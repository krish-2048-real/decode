# WHO Integrated Management of Childhood Illness (IMCI) & Rural Emergency Guidelines

## Red-Flag Emergency Symptoms requiring Immediate 108 Ambulance / PHC Escalation
1. **Gastrointestinal Emergencies**:
   - Vomiting blood (Hematemesis) or passing dark black stool (Melena).
   - Severe, continuous, agonizing abdominal pain with guarding or abdominal rigidity.
   - Profuse watery diarrhea with severe sunken eyes, inability to drink, or lethargy.

2. **Respiratory & Cardiovascular Red Flags**:
   - Severe breathlessness, chest indrawing, stridor at rest, or cyanosis (bluish lips/nails).
   - High fever with stiff neck, altered consciousness, or acute confusion.

3. **Maternal & Obstetric Emergencies**:
   - Severe vaginal bleeding during pregnancy or postpartum.
   - Convulsions/fits during pregnancy (Eclampsia) or severe vision changes with high blood pressure.

4. **Trauma & Environmental Hazards**:
   - Venomous snakebites or rabid animal bites (requires immediate Anti-Snake Venom / Anti-Rabies Vaccine at PHC).
