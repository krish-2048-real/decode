# Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY & National Health Mission (NHM) Operational Guidelines

## Key National Healthcare Schemes in India
1. **Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY**:
   - Coverage: ₹5,00,000 per family per year for secondary and tertiary hospital care.
   - Cashless & Paperless treatment at all impaneled public and private hospitals.
   - Eligibility: Low-income families listed in SECC 2011 / holding BPL or Antyodaya ration cards.

2. **Janani Suraksha Yojana (JSY)**:
   - Cash incentive of ₹1,400 for rural pregnant women for institutional delivery at PHC/Govt Hospital.
   - Free transport (108 Ambulance) to health center and back home.

3. **Pradhan Mantri Matru Vandana Yojana (PMMVY)**:
   - Cash incentive of ₹5,000 for first child in 3 installments to compensate for wage loss during maternity.

4. **Mukhyamantri Vayoshri Yojana & Maharashtra State Health Schemes**:
   - Free health screening, assistive devices, and medical aid for senior citizens above 65 years.
