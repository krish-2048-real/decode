# Arogya Sahayak — Hackathon Presentation & Live Demo Script

## Step-by-Step Demo Walkthrough

### Act 1: Multilingual Clinical Triage & Red-Flag Emergency Escalation (2 Mins)
1. Navigate to the **Symptom Triage** tab.
2. Select language: **मराठी (Marathi)** or **English**.
3. Voice or type critical symptom: *"Severe stomach pain and vomiting blood for the last hour."*
4. Click **Start AI Triage**.
5. Observe:
   - **CRITICAL** banner with immediate first-aid instructions.
   - Escalation rationale: *Vomiting blood (hematemesis) indicates acute gastrointestinal bleeding.*
   - Real-time notification dispatched to ASHA worker desk.

### Act 2: ASHA Field Worker Emergency Alert Dashboard (1 Min)
1. Switch to **ASHA Emergency Alerts** tab.
2. View pending emergency alert dispatched from Khed Sector with anonymized SHA-256 User ID.
3. Click **Acknowledge Alert** -> **Mark Patient Visited**.

### Act 3: Nearest PHC & Hospital Geo-Locator (1 Min)
1. Switch to **Nearest PHC Map** tab.
2. Grant or simulate location (e.g., Pune Rural).
3. View 24x7 Khed Primary Health Centre & Chakan Rural Hospital on Leaflet map with direct call buttons.

### Act 4: Government Scheme Matcher & Printed Village Advisory (1 Min)
1. Switch to **Health Schemes & Bulletin** tab.
2. Select household criteria (BPL card, Pregnant Mother, Senior Citizen).
3. View instant scheme matches (Ayushman Bharat ₹5 Lakh cover, JSY ₹1,400 incentive).
4. Click **Generate Printable Village Health Bulletin** in Marathi/English.
